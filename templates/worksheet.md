# Unconventional Moves worksheet

Copy this into a brief. A normal completed plan has five to seven feasible,
mechanism-distinct moves and exactly one first action. If the goal is unsafe or
the constraints make that impossible, explain the limitation without padding.

## Frame

- Desired outcome and observable success:
- Assumed method and conventional baseline:
- Likely bottleneck (label inference):
- Available time, budget, resources, and time horizon:
- Constraints and non-negotiables:
- Affected people and actual consent status:
- Supplied facts and evidence already checked:
- Material uncertainty or one focused question:

## Move template

Repeat for five to seven genuinely different causal routes. Changing only a
channel, label, or audience does not establish a new mechanism.

### Idea N: Title (mechanism)

- **Concrete move:** Who does what with available resources.
- **Why overlooked:** Hidden assumption or incentive, why it could work here,
  and its main failure condition.
- **48-hour test:** Hypothesis, observation, and decision the evidence changes.
  Starting within 48 hours does not imply proving the whole goal in that time.
- **Success signal:** Observable evidence beyond merely performing the action.
- **Stop condition:** Result, risk, or boundary that ends the test.
- **Evidence status:** Supplied facts, checked facts, inference, or speculation.
- **Bounds:** Meaningful limits on time, cost, exposure, and commitment; rollback.

## Sources, when needed

List checked sources with title, publisher, date when available, URL, and what
each supports. For high-stakes claims without current reliable verification,
write `Verification incomplete` and limit advice to source gathering or
non-consequential steps. Omit this section for low-risk plans without sources.

## Final decision

**Prioritized action:** Exactly one idea, its trade-off against the strongest
alternative, and its first concrete step.

For JSON, follow the bundled schema instead of adding worksheet fields. Use
v0.1 when meaningful numeric baselines are unknown; identify what to measure.
Version 0.2 keeps start within 0 to 48 hours and duration from 1 to 48 hours.
A selected move and a met target never establish approval to execute or continue.
