# Synthetic example prompts

These briefs are entirely fictional. Their constraints are supplied scenario
facts, not claims about real organizations, people, or measured outcomes.

## Partnership activation

> Use $unconventional-moves for a fictional community workshop that wants its existing partner groups to host their first joint session. Two volunteers have three hours this week, no budget, and permission to contact only groups that opted in. Current reminders get polite replies but no dates. Give five distinct mechanisms, compare the ordinary reminder approach, and choose one test to start. Do not treat replies as proof that a session will happen.

## Product adoption

> A fictional shared checklist tool gets trial signups but few completed checklists. We can change copy or setup steps, not build new features this week. We have two staff hours and no reliable conversion baseline. Use $unconventional-moves for six approaches and one recommendation. Separate possible causes from known facts. If using JSON, use the existing contract that can honestly represent missing measurements.

## Distribution

> Use $unconventional-moves to find five practical ways for a fictional free gardening guide to reach readers who will try one technique. We have one afternoon, no ad budget, no audience list, and may post only where sharing is permitted. Distinguish causal mechanisms, not five social channels. Design tests that learn more than whether a link was posted.

## Operational efficiency

> Use $unconventional-moves for a fictional volunteer team whose handoffs repeatedly require clarification. The goal is fewer avoidable interruptions, not simply fewer meetings. Keep urgent escalation and accessibility intact. We can run one reversible trial with consenting volunteers, using existing notes and two hours total. Propose five moves, name the suspected bottleneck, and explain the strongest alternative to the first choice.

## Personal learning

> Use $unconventional-moves to help a fictional beginner recall basic phrases during short conversations despite an irregular schedule. There are fifteen minutes a day, no purchase budget, and no permission to record other people. Generate six distinct approaches. Separate practicing from evidence of recall, say what a two-day test cannot establish, and choose exactly one first action.
